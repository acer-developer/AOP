# FY27 Annual Operating Plan — Board Executive Summary

*Prepared for the Board meeting. All figures from Zoho Books actuals unless marked as assumption.*

## 1. Where we stand today (actuals)

- **Cash position is strong:** ₹26.1 Cr in fixed deposits (₹25.0 Cr in one FD — likely the SEBI CRA net-worth requirement, to confirm), plus ~₹8 L operating cash.
- **Income is currently dominated by FD interest, not operations:** in FY26, 95% of income (₹1.48 Cr) was FD interest; operating rating Sales were only ₹8 L.
- **Operating revenue is turning:** FY27 Q1 Sales already ₹28.5 L — 3.5× the whole of FY26 — a genuine inflection.
- **We run an operating loss:** FY26 net −₹0.88 Cr; FY27 already −₹0.63 Cr in the first ~3.7 months, driven by a rising cost base (salary is ~58% of spend).

## 2. The plan is built around one swing factor: BLR licence timing

Three scenarios, identical cost base, differing only on when BLR revenue starts:

| | S1: BLR by Oct | S2: BLR by Jan | S3: No BLR FY27 |
|--|--:|--:|--:|
| Net P&L (₹ Cr) | −2.87 + BLR(6 mo) | −2.87 + BLR(3 mo) | **−2.87** |

- Common cost base **₹5.34 Cr** (revised, bottom-up: actual payroll + new hires + RC meeting fees + 3 board meetings + one-time IT/branding); locked-in non-BLR income **₹2.47 Cr**.
- **Gap to close: ₹2.87 Cr** — the job of BLR revenue + continued Sales growth.
- **Capacity risk sits alongside the licence-timing risk:** leaders exiting case execution (RC/review only) and IPO volume doubling (10 → ~20 by Oct) draw on the same 5-executor team as BLR. Even S1 (licence by Oct) doesn't guarantee Oct revenue if the team has no free capacity that month.

## 3. Two senior hires added to the plan (payroll impact)

- Combined **₹95 L/year** CTC (one joined 24-Jul, one joins 1-Aug).
- Adds **₹64 L to FY27** and lifts payroll run-rate from ~₹3.0 Cr to ~₹3.95 Cr/year.
- Fully justified if BLR lands early (S1); most "at risk" in S2/S3 — a board decision point.

## 4. What the board is being asked

1. **Note** the financial position and the FD-interest dependence.
2. **Approve** the FY27 cost base including the two hires (or decide to stagger).
3. **Confirm** the BLR revenue assumptions so the scenarios can be finalised.
4. **Align** on the cost-discipline triggers if BLR slips (Scenario 3).

## 5. Open items before finalisation
- [ ] BLR per-mandate revenue + ramp (drives S1/S2 numbers)
- [ ] Confirm ₹25 Cr FD = SEBI net-worth lock (drives true runway)
- [ ] Confirm CTC is fully-loaded cost
- [ ] Any BLR one-time set-up cost / FY27 capex
