03 Strategy

The thesis: BLR is the switch

One regulatory milestone converts idle capital into an operating engine.

1 · The lever

Bank Loan Ratings (RBI ECAI accreditation). Every bank borrower is a candidate — the industry's true volume engine.

2 · The proof

Every small CRA scaled on BLR. Infomerics grew rating revenue ₹64 Cr → ₹95 Cr in three years, BLR-led.

3 · The capacity

Two senior hires (₹95 L/yr) + 4 BDs, put in place ahead of the licence to service mandates from day one.

  • What we will do: invest through FY27 to be ready, win BLR mandates the moment the licence lands, and let recurring surveillance fees compound into a durable annuity.
  • Why it is defensible: we already meet the net-worth bar, we are competitively priced, and the model is industry-validated.

09 Recommendation

Strategy & the ask

What we will do

  • Treat FY27 as the pivot year — invest ahead of BLR, funded by FD interest and reserves.
  • Stand up 4 BDs to a 16-case/month engine, targeting ₹100 Cr+ tickets.
  • Bank the surveillance annuity to reach profitability in FY28.

Guardrails

  • Compliance first. Brickwork lost its licence and ~90% of revenue to process lapses. Rating quality is existential as we scale volume.
  • Cost trigger. If BLR slips past January, stagger the second hire and freeze discretionary spend.
The board is asked to
Approve the FY27 cost base including the two hires · conditional on BLR timing  •  Endorse the 16-case/month BD plan  •  Note that solvency is secure; operational self-sufficiency by FY28 is the goal.
