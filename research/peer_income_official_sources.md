## ⭐ Corrections vs the earlier aggregator figures in this repo

The four unlisted CRAs' numbers previously in [`peer_benchmark_data.csv`](peer_benchmark_data.csv)
and [`cra_peer_financials.md`](cra_peer_financials.md) came from aggregators and are **superseded**
by the official figures above. Notable differences:

| Company | Old (aggregator) | Official (this file) | Note |
|---------|------------------|----------------------|------|
| India Ratings | ₹233.18 Cr (FY25, TheCompanyCheck) | **₹253.79 Cr (FY26)** | official is a later year and higher |
| Infomerics | ₹90.74 Cr ("FY25", Tracxn) | **FY25 ₹77.22 Cr / FY26 ₹98.89 Cr** | aggregator figure did not match the official FY25 disclosure |
| Acuité | ~₹55.01 Cr (FY24, TheCompanyCheck) | **FY26 ₹58.53 Cr / FY25 ₹47.37 Cr** | aggregator period ambiguous; official receipts differ |
| Brickwork | ₹10.9 Cr (Tracxn) | **FY25 ₹6.20 Cr audited / FY26 ₹17.38 Cr unaudited** | aggregator matched no official year |

**Takeaway:** aggregator CRA revenue for unlisted agencies is unreliable — the official
SEBI income disclosures are the authoritative source and are now captured here.

## Data-quality flags
- **ICRA** absolute ratings-segment revenue is **not** published in the press release (growth % only) — left blank rather than derived.
- **India Ratings** income-disclosure page shows **no prior-year comparative**.
- **Infomerics** and **Brickwork FY26** figures are **unaudited**; Brickwork FY25 and Acuité/CARE/CRISIL are audited.
- Periods are **not** uniform (CRISIL CY2024; ICRA FY2025; CARE/India Ratings/Acuité FY2026; Infomerics FY2025-26 unaudited; Brickwork FY2024-25 audited). Compare like-for-like only.
