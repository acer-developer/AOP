# Indian CRA Peer Financials — Verified Benchmark

> **Provenance:** every figure is tied to a primary source (company results PDF,
> BSE/NSE filing, or annual report) that was fetched and read, cross-checked against
> screener.in. Secondary sources are labelled. Figures that could **not** be verified
> are flagged, not guessed. All values INR crore unless stated. Compiled 24-Jul-2026.
>
> **Two source-hygiene rules baked in:**
> 1. CRISIL group revenue ≠ rating revenue — its Ratings segment is only ~28% of group. We use the **Ratings-segment** figure.
> 2. CARE "total income" (incl. other income) ≠ "revenue from operations" — we use revenue from operations.

## 3. Profitability & cost structure (the benchmark that matters for ACER)

| CRA | Operating / EBITDA margin | PAT | Employee cost % of revenue |
|-----|--------------------------:|----:|---------------------------:|
| CRISIL (CY24) | ~27.7% group; **Ratings segment ~55.6%** | 684.07 | ~54% |
| ICRA (FY25) | ~36% | 171.2 | not verified |
| CARE (FY25 consol) | ~39% EBITDA | 140.0 | ~47% |
| CARE (FY25 standalone) | ~46% EBITDA | 147.99 | ~43% |

**Key takeaways for the reality-check:**
1. A mature CRA's **single biggest cost is people (~43–54% of revenue)** — the same shape as ACER, but on a revenue base 50–900× larger.
2. Even carrying that people cost, established CRAs earn **27–56% operating margins**. Ratings is a high-margin business **once revenue scales past the fixed cost base**.
3. The whole game is **getting revenue above the fixed people/compliance cost**. That is precisely ACER's challenge (see [`../board/acer_vs_peers_reality_check.md`](../board/acer_vs_peers_reality_check.md)).

## 4. What is NOT publicly disclosed by ANY peer (so ACER cannot benchmark it)
- **Revenue split by instrument** (bank loan ratings vs bonds/NCD/CP vs securitisation vs surveillance). No listed CRA discloses a rupee split — only qualitative commentary. Do not put a peer BLR-share % in the board deck citing these filings.
- Number of rating assignments / instruments rated (not disclosed numerically).

## 5. Flagged / derived figures (use with care)
- ICRA absolute Ratings-segment **annual** revenue (~₹279 Cr) is **derived** from disclosed growth rates, not a reported number.
- CARE FY24 figures are derived (FY25 ÷ growth) and screener-corroborated, not from a fetched FY24 filing.
- CRISIL and ICRA exact headcounts are third-party estimates, not confirmed from the matching-year annual report.
- Small-CRA net profits and Acuité's first-profitable year could not be verified.
