The annuity is the point

Each rating won is billed once up-front, then every year it stays live. A book built at ₹19,200 Cr/year throws off a ₹2.88 Cr surveillance annuity that compounds — revenue quality improves as the book grows.

These are capacity parameters (16 cases, ₹100 Cr avg, 3 bps + 1.5 bps), i.e. a ceiling at full utilisation — not booked revenue. Actual ramp depends on BLR go-live and mandate wins.

ACER's yearly volume vs the bond market

₹19,200 Cr rated per year against ₹9.9 lakh cr of annual bond issuance alone.

Bond issuance ~₹9.9 lakh cr FY25 (RBI, via press). Bank-loan ratings add a far larger pool on top.

Why the pool is huge

Unlike bonds — only companies tapping capital markets — any company borrowing from a bank is a bank-loan-rating candidate. That is why every small Indian CRA (Infomerics, Acuité, India Ratings) is BLR-led.

The binding constraint is winning mandates against incumbents, not demand. Execution and the BLR licence set the pace.
