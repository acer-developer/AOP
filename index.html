Assumptions and sources

Booked actuals (hard numbers)

  • ACER financials from internal management accounts, about 21-Jul-2026 (FY = Apr to Mar).
  • FD corpus ₹26.1 Cr. FY27 cost base ₹5.27 Cr (₹43.9 L/mo).
  • Non-BLR income ₹2.47 Cr/yr (FD ₹1.50 + sales ₹0.97) = ₹20.6 L/mo.
  • Payroll about 61% of spend. Runway about 8 months on drawable ₹1.9 Cr.

Capacity and model assumptions

  • 16 cases/mo of ₹100 Cr = ₹1,600 Cr/mo, ramping linearly over 6 months post go-live.
  • Fees: IRF 3 bps up-front plus ASF 1.5 bps recurring on the cumulative book.
  • Cost base and non-BLR income held flat. Break-even = first month income at or above expense.
  • Market anchors: bank credit about ₹182 lakh cr (RBI Mar-2025), bond issuance about ₹9.9 lakh cr (FY25). Peer figures search-sourced (official domains were network-blocked this session, reconfirm at source before board sign-off).
