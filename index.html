Bottom line up front

If the BLR licence is live by October, FY27 breaks even and the business flips from interest-led to ratings-led. If it slips, we run a planned, fully funded loss.

08Industry map
We enter a Rs 2,000 crore market at the very bottom, by design.

Established agencies book hundreds of crore a year. ACER starts small and climbs a credible ladder toward the smaller-CRA tier.

Is rating Rs 1,600 Cr a month doable? The market capacity is not the constraint

ACER target: Rs 1,600 Cr rated per month, Rs 19,200 Cr per year, about 192 mandates. Set against the market and what peers already handle, that is a small, credible slice.

The teal sliver is ACER's entire Rs 19,200 Cr yearly target, about 0.27% of outstanding bank credit.
The takeaway. Market capacity is not the constraint. Even one small peer has rated debt a hundred times ACER's yearly target over its life. The binding constraint is winning mandates against seven incumbents, which is why execution and the BLR licence, not demand, decide the pace.

09Execution
A clear path, tracked on hard KPIs, with known risks owned.

Full year of BLR, surveillance annuity, profitability.

KPIs the Board will see

  • Mandates per month and cumulative rated debt.
  • Ratings share of income and revenue versus plan.
  • Cash position and net worth headroom.

Key risks and mitigations

  • BLR licence delay. Cost trigger; tighten discretionary spend if it slips past January.
  • BD ramp shortfall. Disciplined pipeline, phased monthly targets.
  • Competition from incumbents. Price, service, and second-rating niche.
  • Rating quality or compliance failure. Independent rating committee and strong process. Brickwork lost its licence and most of its revenue to lapses, rating revenue fell from about Rs 48.5 Cr to about Rs 5 Cr.
  • Client concentration. Keep no single issuer above 10% of revenue.

Source: Internal [1]. Brickwork history, brickworkratings.com [9].

10Recommendation
Approve an investment year, with a clear trigger if BLR slips.
  • Approve the FY27 operating budget of Rs 5.24 Cr.
  • Endorse the base case: BLR live by 1 October at 16 cases per month, serviced by the four BDs already onboarded.
  • Note the plan is fully funded without touching the Rs 25 Cr net worth, with a cost trigger if BLR slips past January.
The one variable

Solvency is secure. Operational self-sufficiency by FY28 is the goal, and the BLR licence decides the pace.
