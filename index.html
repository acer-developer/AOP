Recommendation

What we will do

  • Treat FY27 as the pivot year, invest ahead of BLR, funded by FD interest and reserves.
  • Stand up 4 BDs to a 16 case per month engine, targeting Rs 100 Cr plus tickets.
  • Bank the surveillance annuity to reach profitability in FY28.

Guardrails

  • Compliance first. Brickwork lost its licence and most of its revenue to process lapses, rating revenue fell from about Rs 48.5 Cr to about Rs 5 Cr. Rating quality is existential as we scale volume.
  • Cost trigger. If BLR slips past January, stagger the second hire and freeze discretionary spend.
The board is asked to
Approve the FY27 cost base including the two hires, conditional on BLR timing. Endorse the 16 case per month BD plan. Note that solvency is secure, and operational self sufficiency by FY28 is the goal.

Source: Brickwork rating revenue history, brickworkratings.com [9]. Hire and cost figures, Zoho Books [1].
