Capacity & model assumptions

  • 16 cases/mo × ₹100 Cr = ₹1,600 Cr/mo, ramping linearly over 6 months post go-live.
  • Fees: IRF 3 bps up-front + ASF 1.5 bps recurring on cumulative book.
  • Cost base & non-BLR income held flat; break-even = first month income ≥ expense.
  • Market anchors: bank credit ~₹182 lakh cr (RBI Mar-2025); bond issuance ~₹9.9 lakh cr (FY25); peer figures search-sourced (official domains network-blocked this session — reconfirm at source before board sign-off).
