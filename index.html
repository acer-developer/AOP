2 · Strategy, and why a borrower picks us

The lever

Bank Loan Ratings, via RBI ECAI accreditation. Every bank borrower is a candidate, the largest addressable pool in the industry.

Why us

Competitive 3 bps pricing, faster turnaround, and demand for a second rating on larger facilities give a new CRA a genuine opening.

The capacity

Two senior hires and four BDs, in place ahead of the licence, so mandates can be serviced from day one.

Source: Infomerics grew rating revenue Rs 64 Cr to Rs 95 Cr in three years [7]. Hiring and pricing, Internal [1] and assumption [2]. Regulatory basis [10].

Full year of BLR, surveillance annuity, profitability.

KPIs the Board will see

  • Mandates per month and cumulative rated debt.
  • Ratings share of income and revenue versus plan.
  • Cash position and net worth headroom.

Key risks and mitigations

  • BLR licence delay. Cost trigger, stagger the second hire, freeze discretionary spend.
  • BD ramp shortfall. Disciplined pipeline, phased monthly targets.
  • Competition from incumbents. Price, service, and second rating niche.
  • Rating quality or compliance failure. Independent rating committee and strong process. Brickwork lost its licence and most of its revenue to lapses, rating revenue fell from about Rs 48.5 Cr to about Rs 5 Cr.
  • Client concentration. Keep no single issuer above 10% of revenue.

Source: Internal [1]. Brickwork history, brickworkratings.com [9].

11 · Recommendation

The Board is asked to approve

  • The FY27 operating budget of Rs 5.24 Cr, including Rs 0.64 Cr for the two new hires.
  • The headcount plan: two senior hires plus four BDs.
  • The base case, BLR live by 1 October at 16 cases per month.
  • Noting the plan is fully funded without touching the Rs 25 Cr net worth, with a cost trigger if BLR slips past January.
Bottom line
Solvency is secure. Operational self sufficiency by FY28 is the goal, and the BLR licence is the one variable that decides the pace.
