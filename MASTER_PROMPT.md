- ACER is a SEBI-registered CRA. Fiscal year runs April to March. FY27 = Apr 2026 to Mar 2027.
- Cash: FD corpus ₹26.1 Cr (₹25.0 Cr in one FD, likely the SEBI net-worth lock; ₹1.11 Cr other FDs; about ₹8 L operating cash).

## THE TEAM AND THE CONSTRAINT
- Team is 2 leaders plus 5 executors. Leaders now sit in Rating Committee and review only, out of case execution. So 5 executors carry all casework.
- One case is one person-week, so real throughput is about 20 cases a month total, shared across the IPO book and the BLR book.
- IPO track has 10 mandates now, ramping to about 20 by October, drawn from the same team as BLR.

## UNIT ECONOMICS (confirmed)
- Income is only two kinds: FD interest and rating fees. Rating splits into IPO and ratings, and BLR once the licence is live.
- FD interest: ₹12.5 L a month (₹1.50 Cr a year), held flat.
- Existing non-IPO rating fees: about ₹4.08 L a month (₹49 L a year).
- IPO mandate fee: ₹1.2 L per quarter per mandate (₹0.4 L a month, ₹4.8 L a year). At 20 mandates that is ₹8 L a month, ₹96 L a year.
- BLR average case size ₹100 Cr. Initial rating fee (IRF) 2.5 bps up-front, which is ₹2.5 L per case. Surveillance fee (ASF) 1.5 bps a year on the cumulative rated book, which is ₹1.5 L a year per case.
- Full BLR capacity is 16 cases a month, ₹1,600 Cr a month, ₹19,200 Cr a year. IRF at full capacity is about ₹40 L a month.

## THE LAUNCH DISCIPLINE (key idea)
The scenarios differ only on when the BLR licence lands. In every BLR-live scenario the go-live month runs ZERO BLR on purpose, because the team must first clear its existing IPO book. BLR then ramps to 16 new cases a month from the very next month. Break-even is the first month total income covers the full expense line.

## THREE DECISIONS THAT CARRY THE YEAR (closing slide)
1. Phase IPO delivery now: commit an October, November, December delivery calendar with clients before the licence lands, so BLR has a lane the day it goes live. Highest leverage move in the plan.
2. Make the analyst hire a rule: add the sixth executor the first time residual capacity turns negative two months running, and only in the BLR-live scenarios. Not on a hunch.
3. Execution is the multiplier: every number assumes IPO and rating cases are delivered on time and to quality. Done well the team compounds into these figures. Slip, and even the BLR scenarios stop reaching break-even.
