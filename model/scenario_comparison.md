# Scenario Comparison — FY27 AOP

> Cost base and non-BLR income are **identical** across scenarios. The only swing
> factor is **when BLR goes live** and therefore how many months of BLR revenue
> land in FY27. BLR revenue shown as `+BLR(n mo)` pending confirmed inputs.

> *Old flat-model expense was ₹5.27 Cr; revised bottom-up build (actual payroll + RC fees + discrete board meetings + IT/branding, consultant fee excluded, insurance dropped) is ₹5.34 Cr — see `01_monthly_budget_fy27.md`.*

## The common backbone (same in all three)
- **Cost base FY27: ₹5.34 Cr** = actual payroll run-rate (existing + 2 new hires) + other opex + RC meeting fees + 3 board meetings + one-time IT/branding.
- **Locked-in non-BLR income: ₹2.47 Cr** = ₹1.50 Cr FD interest + ₹0.97 Cr existing Sales.
- **Structural gap to close: ₹2.87 Cr** — this is what BLR (plus Sales growth) must fill.

## What changes the picture
1. **BLR timing** — each extra quarter of licence ≈ 3 more months of BLR revenue.
2. **New hires** — ₹95 L/yr committed in all scenarios; most "at risk" in S2/S3 where revenue lags the cost.
3. **Existing Sales momentum** — ₹8 L (FY26) → ₹28.5 L (Q1 FY27); if this keeps compounding it independently narrows the gap.
4. **Capacity, not just licence timing** — leaders are moving out of case execution (RC/review only), and IPO volume is doubling (10 → ~20 by Oct) on the *same* 5-executor team that must also carry BLR cases. A scenario "arriving" on the calendar doesn't mean revenue lands if there's no free capacity to execute it — see the capacity/strategy plan.

## Board decision framing
- **If BLR expected early (S1):** proceed with both hires; plan for reduced FY27 loss and FY28 profitability.
- **If BLR uncertain (S2/S3):** decide whether to stagger Hire B and tighten discretionary cost until the licence is secured.
- **Solvency is not the near-term question** (₹26.1 Cr corpus); **operational self-sufficiency is.**

> Populate BLR(6 mo) / BLR(3 mo) once management confirms per-mandate revenue and ramp.
